# Employee Onboarding, Privacy & App Safety Guide

Welcome to the **CA Practice Manager** application! This guide helps you set up the app on your mobile device or computer, explains how it works, and addresses privacy and security details under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**.

---

## 🔒 Part 1: Security & Privacy Reassurance (For Employees)

Your privacy and security are our highest priority. Because this app was built internally for our firm, we want to assure you of its integrity:

1. **No Background Tracking:** The application **never** tracks your location in the background. It only accesses your GPS device for a fraction of a second when you manually click the "Confirm Check-In" button.
2. **Data Retention & Auto-Deletion:** 
   * **Location coordinates** are resolved to a simple distance value (e.g. *"12 meters from office"* or *"At client site"*) and deleted immediately from the database once verified.
   * **Selfies** are stored securely with strict encryption and are **automatically deleted after 90 days**.
3. **No Spyware / Malicious Code:** The application contains no ads, no trackers, and no third-party data sharing libraries. It is a utility built purely to replace manual registers.
4. **Access Control:** Your selfies and check-in records are protected by **Row-Level Security**. This means other employees cannot view your data; it is only visible to you and the authorized HR/Partner who signs off on payroll.

## 🎙️ Part 2: Onboarding Video & Audio Voiceover Script
*If you are creating a training video or recording a voice note for staff, you can read this script aloud at a normal, conversational speed.*

### **Slide 1: Introduction**
* **Visual on screen:** App welcome screen and firm logo.
* **Voiceover:** 
  > "Hello team! Welcome to the training walkthrough for our new CA Practice Manager application. This app is designed to make recording your daily attendance, assignments, and weekly timesheets simple and fast—whether you are working at our head office or out on-site conducting a client audit. 
  > 
  > Before we begin, we want to assure you that this app has been built internally with high data privacy standards in mind. It does not track your location in the background, does not share your data, and is fully compliant with India’s DPDP Act. Let's walk through how to install it on your device in under two minutes."

### **Slide 2: How to Access the App**
* **Visual on screen:** Phone screen typing the server IP address or domain name into Google Chrome or Apple Safari.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "First, open the web browser on your smartphone. If you are connected to the office network, type in the IP address provided by HR. If you are working remotely from a client site, turn on your Tailscale VPN app first, or navigate to our firm's public web link. 
  > 
  > You will see a clean login screen. Go ahead and enter your email address and password. For employees, there is no extra two-factor code required, so you can log in instantly."

### **Slide 3: Installing the App (PWA)**
* **Visual on screen:** Chrome menu opening, tapping "Install App" on Android. Or Safari share sheet scrolling down to tap "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Because this is a Progressive Web App, you don't need to download it from the App Store. 
  > 
  > If you are on Android, tap the three dots in the top right corner of Chrome and select 'Install app'. 
  > 
  > If you are on an iPhone, open Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom—which looks like a square with an up arrow—scroll down and tap 'Add to Home Screen'. 
  > 
  > A native icon will appear directly on your home screen. Tap it to run the app in beautiful full-screen mode, just like any other mobile app."

### **Slide 4: Clocking In (Office vs. Client Site)**
* **Visual on screen:** The Employee Attendance screen showing "Office Check-In" and "Client Site Audit" buttons.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Once you open the app, tap 'Clock In Today'. You will see two options: 'Office Check-In' and 'Client Site Audit'. 
  > 
  > If you are working at the Indiranagar Head Office, select 'Office Check-In'. 
  > 
  > If you are traveling for an offsite audit, select 'Client Site Audit' and choose your client from the dropdown list. 
  > 
  > Next, scroll down, take a quick verification selfie, check the DPDP consent box to permit secure location validation, and tap 'Confirm Check-In'. You are all set! Your attendance goes straight to HR for review."

### **Slide 5: Logging Weekly Timesheets**
* **Visual on screen:** Timesheet grid showing Monday to Sunday hours input.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Finally, remember to log your billable hours! Tap the 'Timesheets' tab, select the client assignment you worked on, enter the hours you spent each day, add a brief description of the work, and tap 'Save'. This ensures your client billing is accurate and helps us track assignment completion. 
  > 
  > Thank you for watching this guide. If you have any technical difficulties, please reach out to the HR team. Have a great day!"
